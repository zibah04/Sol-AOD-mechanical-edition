# Mechanical Edition — visual specification

## Composition
- Canvas basis: 1080 px wide MAML layout; adapt and verify on device.
- Background: pure black (the AOD canvas is intended to remain transparent/black).
- Mechanical dial: oversized on the left and deliberately cropped by the left edge.
- Main time: prominent hours with minutes inside a champagne-gold capsule.
- Secondary information: weekday, date, and battery percentage.

## Palette
- Champagne gold: `#C6A15B`
- Near-black: `#080808`
- Mechanical graphite: `#202020` to `#454545`
- Secondary text: muted gray.

## Restraint
The dial must remain visually quieter than the time. Avoid bright white outlines, excessive glow, or large filled areas. Keep the non-black pixel area within Xiaomi's published AOD review guidance.

## Known design limitation
The current MAML circles and lines are a structural placeholder for the mechanical dial, not the final precision artwork. The composition and coordinates must be previewed and adjusted in the Xiaomi AOD Editor and on the target phone.
